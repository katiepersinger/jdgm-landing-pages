Why we call it the bullpen

For years, whenever Pastor J.D. prepares a message, he writes far more than he can actually say. The extra illustrations, the rabbit trails, the good questions he doesn't have time to chase, the ideas that don't make the final cut — all of it gets kept in one section of his notes he calls the bullpen.

This podcast is built around those conversations. After J.D. preaches, he sits down with a few friends to unpack the message further — going deeper into the passage, wrestling with the hard questions, and exploring the implications there simply wasn't room to address on Sunday.

It isn't a sermon recap or a scripted teaching podcast. It's a free-flowing conversation about Scripture, theology, culture, and the questions Christians are actually wrestling with.
